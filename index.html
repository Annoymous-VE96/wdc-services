Enquiries

Frequent questions.

Still stuck on something? Call Dom on 0425 801 004 and get a straight answer.

Are quotes really free?
Yes. Dom comes out, measures up, and talks through layout and materials, then sends you a fixed written quote. There is no charge and no obligation. If we think the job is not worth doing, or not worth doing yet, we will tell you that too.
Where are you based and where do you work?
We are based in Balwyn North and cover Melbourne's inner east: Doncaster, Templestowe, Kew, Balwyn, Blackburn, Mitcham, Vermont, Bulleen, Ivanhoe, Donvale, Viewbank and Nunawading. We take on most Melbourne metro jobs as well, so it is worth asking.
Do you take on small jobs?
We do. Plenty of our work is a single laundry, a split system install, or a bathroom that just needs a leak sorted properly. Give us a call and we will tell you straight away whether we are the right fit for it.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Our plumbers, electricians, tilers and waterproofers are all licensed, every wet area gets certified waterproofing, and all our work comes with a warranty for peace of mind.
How long will my bathroom take?
Most bathrooms run two to three weeks from strip-out to handover. We book every trade in before we start, so you are not sitting on a stripped bathroom waiting a week for a tiler to become available.
Can you handle an insurance claim?
Yes. We have done a lot of insurance work, including water damage from leaks. We can quote in the format your insurer needs and deal with them directly. Sometimes people do not realise they can claim at all, so it is worth a conversation before you pay out of pocket.
